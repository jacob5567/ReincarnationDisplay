# Changelog

## 1.0.2
- The panel no longer covers the count shown above a page in the N'tormat page selector when you hold more than one of it.
- `PanelOffsetY` now applies only when the page selector is directly below the panel. Above a page count, or above the N'tormat icon when there is no selector, the panel keeps a small fixed gap.

## 1.0.1
- New icon: the Reincarnation page inside a sacrifice zone ring.
- The mod's entry in the Risk Of Options settings menu shows the icon.

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- World rings mark every valid sacrifice zone along the ground, leaving out stretches over rocks and drops.
- A hint above the N'tormat icon says when the stage has a sacrifice zone.
- Standing in a zone shows a panel with where the sacrifice leads (named only when guaranteed), whether the stage count stays the same, and a countdown for the stage-start reroll window.
- A held page disabled by Hex of Loss is flagged in red.
- Settings in the config file, or in game with Risk Of Options.
